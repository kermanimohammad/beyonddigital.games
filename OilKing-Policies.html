Privacy Policy
Oil King

Effective Date:  July 1, 2026


This Privacy Policy explains how Ashkan Haji ("we", "us", or "the Developer") handles information in the mobile game Oil King.
By using Oil King, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
1. Developer Information
Developer: Ashkan Haji
Game: Oil King
Contact Email: studiohypergame@gmail.com
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information in Oil King, you can contact us at:
studiohypergame@gmail.com
2. Information We Collect
Oil King does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or email address to play the game.
Game progress and certain game settings may be stored locally on the user's device.
However, Oil King uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically.
Advertising Information
Oil King uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.
Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:
• Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers
• IP address
• Device and technical information
• Information about interactions with advertisements
• Diagnostic and performance information
• Information used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention
The collection and processing of this information is handled according to Google's applicable privacy practices and policies.
For more information, please review Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
3. In-App Purchases
Oil King offers optional in-app purchases.
Payments are processed through the applicable Google Play billing system. We do not directly collect or store users' payment card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials.
Information required to process an in-app purchase may be handled by Google Play according to Google's policies and terms.
4. How We Use Information
Information handled through the game and its third-party services may be used for purposes including:
• Providing and maintaining the game
• Saving and restoring game progress locally on the device
• Displaying advertisements
• Measuring advertising performance
• Preventing fraud and abuse
• Diagnosing technical problems and improving game performance
• Processing in-app purchases
We do not use the information collected through the game to personally identify users.
5. Data Storage
Game progress and game settings are currently stored locally on the user's device.
We do not currently operate a user account system or a server-based system for storing individual game save data.
If we introduce additional online services, cloud saves, analytics, account systems, or other data-processing features in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices.
6. Third-Party Services
Oil King may use third-party services that process information as part of providing their services.
Currently, these services include:
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
Used to provide advertisements and related advertising services.
Google Play Billing
Used to process optional in-app purchases.
These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.
7. Children's Privacy
Oil King is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at:
studiohypergame@gmail.com
We will take appropriate steps to address the issue.
8. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Oil King.
Data transmitted by third-party services may be protected using security technologies provided by those services, including encryption during transmission where applicable.
However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
9. Data Retention and Deletion
Game save data stored locally by Oil King can generally be removed by deleting the game's local data or uninstalling the game from the user's device.
Because Oil King does not currently require user accounts, we do not maintain user account data that can be deleted through an account-deletion system.
Information processed by third-party services, such as advertising or payment services, is subject to the respective third party's data retention and deletion policies.
10. Internet Connection
Oil King may require an internet connection for certain features, including displaying advertisements and processing online services.
The game may function without an internet connection for features that do not require online services.
11. Future Changes
We may update Oil King in the future by adding new features or third-party services, such as analytics, cloud services, account systems, or other functionality.
If our data practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.
The updated version will be made available through the Privacy Policy location associated with Oil King.
12. Your Privacy Choices
Depending on your device, operating system, and location, you may have options to manage advertising identifiers and certain privacy settings through your device settings or Google's services.
You may also choose not to use features that require an internet connection.
13. Contact Us
If you have any questions, requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Oil King's privacy practices, please contact:
Ashkan Haji
Email: studiohypergame@gmail.com
