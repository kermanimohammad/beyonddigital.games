Privacy Policy
UP

Effective Date:  September 11, 2026


Ashkan Haji ("we", "our", or "us") developed UP as a free mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use UP.
1. Information We Collect
UP does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.
The game does not require users to:
• Create an account
• Log in or register
• Provide an email address
• Provide their name, phone number, or other personal information
• Provide location information
UP does not use analytics, advertising, or other services to collect user information.
2. Local Game Data
UP may store game progress and other gameplay-related data locally on the user's device.
This data is used solely to provide the game's save and gameplay functionality. We do not receive or have access to this locally stored data.
If the user uninstalls the game or clears the application's data, locally stored game progress may be deleted.
3. Advertising
UP does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services or advertising identifiers.
4. In-App Purchases
UP does not offer in-app purchases or paid digital products within the game.
5. Third-Party Services
UP does not currently use third-party services for advertising, analytics, authentication, social features, or user data collection.
The game does not use services or SDKs such as:
• Firebase
• Unity Analytics
• Unity Ads
• Google Play Games Services
• Facebook/Meta SDK
• GameAnalytics
• Sentry
6. Children's Privacy
UP is intended for users of all ages and does not have an age restriction.
Because UP does not collect personal information from users, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.
7. Data Security
Because UP does not collect or transmit personal information to us, we do not store users' personal information on our servers.
Gameplay data stored locally by the game remains on the user's device.
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the game's features or data practices change.
Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
